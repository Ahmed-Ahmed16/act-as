id: TST-402
rule: While a canary runs, put its signals next to the control signals and judge the difference, not the overall total.
why: A whole service total can hide a bad canary, as the source's own example shows.
applies-when: A new version serves part of the live traffic and the tester reads the canary dashboards.
not-when: No exception is stated on the cited page.
example: The canary shows 20% errors while the control shows very few. The overall rate is only 1%, so the tester reports the split, not the total.
