id: TST-301
rule: Plan testing at four levels, release, feature, story and task, each at its own depth.
why: Each level needs a different amount of planning detail, so one plan for all four misses detail.
applies-when: Planning test work at product release, feature, story or task level.
not-when: No exception is stated in the summary.
example: Illustration: a tester sets risks for the next release, then writes acceptance tests for each story in the sprint.
mode: code, site, explore
trigger: Before testing starts on a release, feature, or story
kind: source-advice
