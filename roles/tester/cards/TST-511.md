id: TST-511
rule: Keep bugs in separate reports when they need different tests to verify their fixes.
why: If 1 report holds several bugs, some may never get fixed.
applies-when: Deciding whether a report combines several bugs: compare the tests needed to verify each fix.
not-when: No exception is stated in the lesson.
example: A login page that times out and also shows wrong error text needs 2 reports, because the fix and the retest are different.
