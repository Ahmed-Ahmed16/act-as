rule: Base the automation strategy on the testing requirements, the product architecture and the skills of the available staff.
why: These factors shape what can be automated and which tools fit; manual testing may be enough for some parts.
applies-when: When a team proposes adding automation or a new automation tool.
not-when: The lesson notes that manual testing may be good enough for some parts of the product.
example: A team with no programmers picks a tool that needs code to maintain. The plan should match the skills the team has, or nobody will be able to maintain the suite.
