id: TST-205
rule: Put most automated checks at the unit and component layer, and keep fewer checks at each higher layer.
why: Unit and component tests are the quickest and least expensive to write, and give the quickest feedback.
applies-when: Deciding which layer a new automated check belongs in, or a suite has grown heavy at the top.
not-when: The behavior only exists in the full user path, such as a browser flow; that check belongs at the top and should stay small in number.
example: A price rule gets a unit test on the pricing function, and one browser check confirms the price shows on the page.
mode: code, site
trigger: deciding which layer an automated check belongs in
