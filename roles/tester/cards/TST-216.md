id: TST-216
rule: Use a defect tracking system when the team is distributed, needs audit or release records, has bugs that escape an iteration, or maintains a legacy system with many defects.
why: A card on a board or a talk does not keep a record for someone who was not in the room.
applies-when: The team is distributed, needs audit or release notes, has bugs that escape an iteration, or maintains a legacy system with many defects.
not-when: A disciplined agile team fixes bugs within the iteration, or only needs story board visibility; index cards may be enough.
example: A bug found by a remote team in the test environment is logged with its steps, so the release notes can list it as known.
