id: TST-012
rule: Write a repro test from the report's steps, and check that it fails on the bug's assertion.
why: A repro test that fails for the right reason is the evidence that the finding is a real bug.
applies-when: a finding is being verified as a bug
not-when: a finding with no reproduction steps yet, which needs steps first
example: a report says the total is wrong after a discount, so the repro test checks that total and fails on it
mode: explore
trigger: when verifying a candidate bug from a bug bash
