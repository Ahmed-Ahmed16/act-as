id: TST-513
rule: Use neutral language, and avoid blaming or talking down to the people involved.
why: A blaming report loses credibility and makes the fix less likely.
applies-when: When a report describes a mistake someone made or a choice someone defended.
not-when: No exception is stated in the lesson.
example: Not "The developer forgot the null check again." Instead: "Saving with an empty address field crashes the page."
