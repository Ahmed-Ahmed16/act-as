id: TST-504
rule: Recognise that every test relies on a model of the product, and a flawed model produces flawed tests.
why: Your tests inherit the blind spots of your picture of the product, not the product itself.
applies-when: When you choose what to cover for a feature.
not-when: No exception is stated in the lesson.
example: The tester's model of a signup form has only email and password. No test ever touches the optional phone field, which the real form checks.
