id: TST-410
rule: Release toggles should generally be retired within about 1 or 2 weeks, but product focused toggles can stay longer.
why: Release toggles are transitional, and expiry details can show which toggles were meant to be short lived.
applies-when: Reviewing the age or expiry details of a release toggle after rollout.
not-when: A product focused toggle; do not treat 1 or 2 weeks as a strict deadline for it.
example: The tester reports a release toggle still present well beyond about 1 or 2 weeks to its owner, with its expiry details if available.
mode: code
trigger: during a flag review on a test copy, before a release is cleared
