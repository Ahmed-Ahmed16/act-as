id: TST-501
rule: Let your mission decide what you test first and how deep you go.
why: A tester with no stated mission spends effort on whatever looks interesting, not on what the project needs.
applies-when: At the start of a test effort, or when you cannot say why a test is worth running.
not-when: No exception is stated in the lesson.
example: A team asks for a quality assessment before release. The mission is an assessment, so the tester runs broad risk tests across the main flows, not a deep dive into 1 rarely used screen.
