id: TST-503
rule: When the spec does not give the expected result, work it out from clues before you judge the output.
why: Test design often needs inference, because authoritative guidance on what to test or expect is rare, and the guidance that exists needs interpretation.
applies-when: When the spec is missing, vague or out of date, or when you write the expected result yourself.
not-when: No exception is stated in the lesson.
example: A discount page shows a total, and no rule says how to round. The tester works out the total from the price, tax and discount rules, and a gap of 1 cent shows the rounding is wrong.
mode: code, site, explore
trigger: when writing the expected result for a new check
