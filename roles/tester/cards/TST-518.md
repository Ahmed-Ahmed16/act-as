id: TST-518
rule: Do not use bug counts as the main measure of project progress.
why: A large count of open bugs can show the product is far from ready, but a falling count does not prove it is near release.
applies-when: When you report progress to managers or judge whether the product is close to ready.
not-when: The lesson says bug counts are useful for showing that the project is far from its release.
example: A falling bug count can mean testers ran fewer tests, not that the product became more stable.
