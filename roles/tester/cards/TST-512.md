id: TST-512
rule: Give the summary line enough detail for managers to decide whether to ask for more information.
why: Managers may dismiss weak summary lines during triage.
applies-when: When you write the title of a bug report.
not-when: No exception is stated in the lesson.
example: "Checkout fails" is weak. "Checkout fails when a coupon and a second item are both in the cart" lets a manager see the cause and the reach.
