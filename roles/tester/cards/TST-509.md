id: TST-509
rule: Set severity from the consequences, and keep it separate from priority, which can change as the project goes on.
why: Mixing the two makes a harmless bug look urgent, or a serious bug look minor, for the wrong reason.
applies-when: When you fill in the severity field of a bug report.
not-when: No exception is stated in the lesson.
example: A footer typo is low priority to fix now, but a bug that loses saved data is severe whatever the schedule says.
mode: site, code, explore
trigger: before setting severity on a bug report
