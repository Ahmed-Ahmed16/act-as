id: TST-413
rule: Judge a release by comparing the new and old versions over the same time window, not the hours before and after the change.
why: The source says time is one of the biggest sources of change in metrics, so a before and after comparison mixes in normal change.
applies-when: The tester is checking whether a release made a metric worse.
not-when: No exception is stated on the cited page.
example: A Monday release is compared with the weekend before it, and the drop in traffic is blamed on the release. The tester compares canary and control at the same hour instead.
mode: site
trigger: when judging a release from metrics across two time windows
