id: TST-505
rule: Ask yourself what question should guide the test before you run it.
why: A test without a question tells you little, and you may not notice what you were really asking.
applies-when: When you design a test, or when you pick up a test whose purpose is unclear.
not-when: The lesson says it can be fine to skip this when you are only looking for the obvious problems.
example: Before testing an export button, the tester writes the question: does the CSV file hold every row shown on screen?
