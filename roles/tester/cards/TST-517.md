id: TST-517
rule: Stop when the clients have enough evidence to make a good decision, using judgement rather than a formula.
why: No formula reliably says when testing is enough, so the judgment has to be about the decision in front of the client.
applies-when: When someone asks whether testing is finished, or when you decide to stop testing.
not-when: No exception is stated in the lesson.
example: A release call depends on the payment flow. Once the payment results and the known risks are reported, testing is enough for that call, even if some screens are untested.
