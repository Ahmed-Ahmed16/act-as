id: REV-342
rule: Logs are kept for the period the project needs and are not kept beyond it.
why: OWASP says log data and its copies must not be kept beyond the required retention period, and that legal and contract duties can set that period.
applies-when: A change adds a log file, log channel, backup of logs or a log setting such as days to keep.
not-when: The change leaves the retention of existing logs alone.
example: In invented PR 101 for owner/shop, the new channel uses the single driver with no daily rotation or days limit. The file grows for ever and keeps customer data for years.
mode: security, full
trigger: When a change adds or changes how long logs are kept
