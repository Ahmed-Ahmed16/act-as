id: REV-232
rule: Put area: <name> on every business entry, in every field of the packet and in the check record.
why: A rerun replaces the entries of 1 area, and it can only find them by this field. Rule ids such as R3 repeat across pages.
applies-when: Writing any business entry in the packet or the check record.
not-when: none
example: In invented PR 101 for owner/shop, the Known risks entry for R3 carries "area: ordering". The Pointers entry for R10 carries the same field.
mode: business, full
trigger: When writing a business entry
kind: user-policy
source: user decision, reviewer v1 spec, the packet format (business entries carry area), approved 2026-10-25
