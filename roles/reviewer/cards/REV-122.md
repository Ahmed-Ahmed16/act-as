id: REV-122
rule: Follow a route to its handler by its form. [Class::class, 'method'] names the method. Inside Route::controller(Class::class) a bare method name is a method of that class. Route::resource makes the conventional resource actions. Route::view names a view.
why: Laravel documents each form. Reading the form correctly is how a changed controller method is tied to the route, and so to the entry point, that reaches it.
applies-when: The split step traces a changed controller method or view to the routes that reach it (full mode step 2, sub step c).
not-when: The route handler is a closure or a single action controller. This card does not cover them; read the closure in place.
example: In invented PR 101 for owner/shop, routes/web.php has Route::controller(RefundController::class)->group(...) with Route::post('/refunds', 'store'). The bare name store means RefundController::store, so a change to that method belongs to the POST /refunds entry point.
mode: full
trigger: When tracing a route to the controller method or view it runs
