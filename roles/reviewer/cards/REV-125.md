id: REV-125
rule: A scheduled item is named in routes/console.php, or in withSchedule in bootstrap/app.php. Schedule::command, Schedule::job and Schedule::call each name one. A schedule line and the command it runs are 1 entry point.
why: Laravel says the task schedule is typically defined in routes/console.php, and that withSchedule in bootstrap/app.php can hold it instead. Reading both places finds every scheduled item.
applies-when: The split step lists the scheduled jobs of a Laravel project to find entry points (full mode step 2, sub step b).
not-when: none
example: In invented PR 101 for owner/shop, routes/console.php has Schedule::command('reports:weekly')->weekly(). The command class and its schedule line form 1 group, named by the weekly report command.
mode: full
trigger: When listing the scheduled jobs that really load
