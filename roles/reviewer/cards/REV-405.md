id: REV-405
rule: A class that PHPMD's ExcessiveClassLength flags is a pointer with 1 line of judgement. It is never a finding by itself. The tool measures 1 class, not a whole file.
why: PHPMD says a long class may be trying to do too much. That is a hint to look, not proof.
applies-when: PHPMD reports ExcessiveClassLength on a changed PHP file.
not-when: none
example: In invented PR 101 for owner/shop, PHPMD flags a 1200 line class. The reviewer reads it and finds it holds orders, invoices and exports. The pointer says "1200 line class: problem, 3 jobs (question 11; question 12 too once its card loads)".
