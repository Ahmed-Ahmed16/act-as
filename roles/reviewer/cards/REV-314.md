id: REV-314
rule: Store an upload on a disk that fits who may read it. The public disk is for files anyone may fetch; other files go to a private disk outside the web root.
why: Laravel says the public disk is meant for files that are publicly accessible. OWASP says to keep uploads outside the web root or on a separate host, and not executable by the server.
applies-when: A changed line stores an uploaded file, or a filesystem disk is added or changed.
not-when: Every file in the upload is meant to be public, such as a product photo.
example: In invented PR 101 for owner/shop, customer ID scans are saved with store('ids', 'public'). They sit under public/storage, so anyone with the link can open them. They belong on a private disk.
mode: security, full
trigger: When a changed line picks the disk for an upload
