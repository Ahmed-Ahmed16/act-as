id: REV-205
rule: If a page has no Covers line within the first 5 lines, do not apply it. Write "not checked: page has no Covers line" and make that area incomplete.
why: Without the line, the reviewer would have to guess which files the page covers, and a guessed mapping must never decide a verdict.
applies-when: A rules page of a touched area has no Covers line in its first 5 lines.
not-when: none
example: In invented PR 101 for owner/shop, shipping.md starts with a title and 12 lines of rules, with no Covers line. The reviewer writes "not checked: page has no Covers line" and the shipping area is incomplete.
mode: business, full
trigger: When a rules page has no Covers line
