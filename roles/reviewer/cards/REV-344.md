id: REV-344
rule: After a valid signature, still compare the order id, amount and currency in the callback with our own record of the order.
why: OWASP says a valid signature shows who sent the payload, not that its contents are safe. The Business Logic sheet says amounts are computed on the server from your own data and not taken from the client.
applies-when: A change adds or edits a webhook or payment callback handler that uses order id, amount or currency.
not-when: The handler does not use an order, amount or currency.
example: In invented PR 101 for owner/shop, the handler marks order 40 paid when the callback says paid. It never compares the amount, so a callback for a 1 unit payment marks a 100 unit order paid.
mode: security, full
trigger: When a callback handler uses order id, amount or currency
