id: REV-504
rule: Prefer a change that solves the problem the team knows about now, not one that might come later.
why: Google says the future problem should be solved once it arrives and its real shape is known, and Fowler says a feature guessed early is likely to be wrong.
applies-when: The PR adds options, hooks or generality for a case that no current task, ticket or caller asks for.
not-when: The PR text or the task names the future need and a date or owner for it.
example: In invented PR 101 for owner/shop, the cart discount PR also adds a per country tax table that no task mentions. The reviewer writes it as a future problem and asks for it to wait.
