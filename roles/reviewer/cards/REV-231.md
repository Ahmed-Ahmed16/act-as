id: REV-231
rule: Build the business verdict from every touched area: fail if any area fails; else incomplete if any area is not checked or depends on something; else pass. Use the verdict tool's line as it is.
why: One bad area must not be hidden by a clean one, and an unchecked area must not read as a pass.
applies-when: Writing the business verdict of a run or a rerun.
not-when: none
example: In invented PR 101 for owner/shop, ordering fails and shipping is incomplete. The business line is fail. If ordering passed and shipping were incomplete, it would be incomplete.
mode: business, full
trigger: When building the business verdict
