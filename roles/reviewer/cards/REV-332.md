id: REV-332
rule: List the proxies your app trusts by address. Use * to trust all proxies only when you cannot know the balancer addresses.
why: Laravel lets you name the load balancers or proxies the app trusts, and offers * for cloud balancers whose addresses you do not know. It also lets you choose which proxy headers are trusted.
applies-when: A change touches trustProxies, TrustProxies middleware, or bootstrap/app.php proxy settings.
not-when: The app is not behind a proxy or load balancer.
example: In invented PR 101 for owner/shop, bootstrap/app.php gets trustProxies(at: '*') on a server that is reachable directly. A visitor can send a forged X-Forwarded-For header and the app believes it.
mode: security, full
trigger: When a change sets which proxies the app trusts
