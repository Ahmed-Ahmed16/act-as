id: REV-112
rule: When you ask for a split, each part must leave the system working: it builds and passes its own tests.
why: Google says the whole system must keep working after each part is submitted, so a part that breaks the build is not a valid split.
applies-when: The reviewer proposes or judges a split of a PR into parts.
not-when: none
example: In invented PR 101 for owner/shop, a proposed part adds a controller that calls a service the next part creates. That part does not work alone, so the split is moved.
mode: size, full
trigger: When proposing or judging a split into parts
