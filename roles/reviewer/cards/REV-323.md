id: REV-323
rule: A login, password reset, 2 factor or callback route has a rate limit or another limit on repeated tries.
why: Laravel attaches rate limits to routes with the throttle middleware. OWASP asks for login throttling, a per account rate limit on reset requests, a rate limit on push prompts for a second factor, and limits on a webhook endpoint.
applies-when: A change adds or edits a login, logout, password reset, 2 factor, payment or callback route, or removes a throttle from one.
not-when: A rate limit is set for the whole route group and the reviewer has read it.
example: In invented PR 101 for owner/shop, the PR adds POST /password/email with no throttle. Anyone can send thousands of reset mails to 1 address.
mode: security, full
trigger: When a changed route is a login, reset, 2 factor, payment or callback route
