id: REV-237
rule: If a rerun added a page that says who may do what, apply the security line rule: pass becomes incomplete with "merge only after: rerun security for <area>", incomplete keeps its reason and gains that one, fail stays fail and gains "also: rerun security for <area>". A line that says not asked or not checked stays as it is.
why: Security read the code without that page. Its answer may change once the rules about who may touch what are known.
applies-when: A business rerun added a page that says who may do what.
not-when: none
example: In invented PR 101 for owner/shop, the new gifts page says only staff may cancel an order. The security pass becomes incomplete with "merge only after: rerun security for gifts".
