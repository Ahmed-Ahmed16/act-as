id: REV-134
rule: A part that needs 2 or more parts waits until every one of them is merged into main. Then it opens against main. It never sits on another part's branch.
why: The user settled this in amendment B24: every split part opens against main after the parts it needs are merged. A part on another part's branch can sit on only 1 of its needs, and it may get no CI there.
applies-when: A part in the split plan has 2 or more entries in its needs list (full mode step 2, sub step g).
not-when: The part needs 0 or 1 parts. With 0 it opens against main at once. With 1 it waits until that part is merged, then opens against main.
example: In invented PR 101 for owner/shop, part 4 needs part 2 and part 3. Part 2 and part 3 are merged first, in any order. Part 4 then opens against main, not against part 3's branch.
mode: full
trigger: When a part needs 2 or more other parts
