id: REV-137
rule: A part may stay over 300 counted lines only for 1 new class or method that is over 300 counted lines alone. Name it, give its counts, and leave the decision to the user.
why: The user wants the cap to hold for every part except one case that cannot be cut. A quiet exception would hide a large part. Naming it lets the user choose to review it or send it back.
applies-when: A part of group 1 cannot get under 300 counted lines (full mode step 2, sub step h).
not-when: The part is over 300 for any other reason, such as many unrelated pieces. Then move the unrelated work out.
example: In invented PR 101 for owner/shop, RefundCalculator is a new class of 340 counted lines. Its part stays over the cap, with the declaration and closing lines recorded and the line "Your call: review it anyway or send it back".
mode: full
trigger: When a part cannot get under 300 counted lines
