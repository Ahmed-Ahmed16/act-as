id: REV-202
rule: Name a rules page in the words the business uses for the area, not in code names.
why: The spec wants the PR author to find the page for their area by its name, and an author knows the business words.
applies-when: Naming a draft rules page, or judging whether a page name fits its area.
not-when: none
example: In invented PR 101 for owner/shop, a draft page is named ordering-and-cancelling.md. It is not named OrderController.md.
mode: business, full
trigger: When naming or judging a rules page
kind: user-policy
source: user decision, reviewer v1 spec, the business rules pages, user story 64, approved 2026-10-25
