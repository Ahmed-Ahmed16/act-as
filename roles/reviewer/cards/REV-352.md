id: REV-352
rule: A database transaction does not make an outside charge safe. Use the provider's idempotency key, and plan what to do when the result of the outside call is unknown.
why: OWASP says a database transaction in our own system cannot make an outside action (a charge, a refund, a message or a booking) and the saving of its result succeed together or fail together. It says to send the same idempotency key again on a retry, and to find out what happened to an unknown result before sending again.
applies-when: A change calls an outside service that charges, refunds, sends or books something, inside or next to a database transaction.
not-when: The outside call changes nothing.
example: In invented PR 101 for owner/shop, the code charges the card inside a transaction, then saves the order. The save fails and the transaction rolls back, but the charge stays. A retry charges again.
mode: security, full
trigger: When a change makes an outside call that has an effect
