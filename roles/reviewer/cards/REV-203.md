id: REV-203
rule: Look for the Covers: line within the first 5 lines of a rules page. A title line and a blank line may sit above it.
why: Ahmed settled in B15 that the line may sit in the first 5 lines, so a normal page title does not hide it.
applies-when: Reading a rules page to find which files it covers.
not-when: none
example: In invented PR 101 for owner/shop, the page has a title on line 1, a blank line 2, and Covers: on line 3. The line counts. On a page where Covers: first appears on line 9, it does not.
mode: business, full
trigger: When reading the Covers line of a page

source: user decision, reviewer v1 spec, amendment B15 (Ahmed, 2026-10-27)
