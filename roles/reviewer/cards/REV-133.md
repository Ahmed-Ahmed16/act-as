id: REV-133
rule: Order the parts by need. A part works once the parts below it are in. It never needs a part above it.
why: Google says the whole system must keep working after each change is submitted, and the Linux kernel guide says the same about each patch in a series. A part that needs a later part breaks the build until that part lands.
applies-when: The split step writes the need links between groups and orders them (full mode step 2, sub step g).
not-when: none
example: In invented PR 101 for owner/shop, the export part calls RefundService::total, which the refund part adds. The refund part comes first, and the export part lists it under needs.
mode: full
trigger: When ordering the groups or parts of a split plan
kind: source-advice
source: Google eng-practices, small CLs page, Don't Break the Build, https://google.github.io/eng-practices/review/developer/small-cls.html#break, read 2026-10-28 ; second source: Linux kernel documentation, Submitting patches, https://www.kernel.org/doc/html/latest/process/submitting-patches.html, read 2026-10-28
