id: REV-136
rule: Money, access and deletion each stand alone as their own part. Mark such a piece money, access or deletion, or unsure when you cannot tell.
why: The user wants a change that can move money, grant access or delete data to be reviewed by itself, so nothing else in the part hides it.
applies-when: The split step cuts group 1 into parts and finds a piece that touches amounts, charges, refunds, who may do what, or removal of data (full mode step 2, sub step h).
not-when: none
example: In invented PR 101 for owner/shop, a refund amount change sits among screen changes in group 1. It becomes its own part, marked money, and the plan says it needs the separate Opus review.
mode: full
trigger: When cutting group 1 into parts and a piece touches money, access or deletion
