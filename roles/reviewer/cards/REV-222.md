id: REV-222
rule: For a rule changed together with its code, add a Not checked line "approval: not checked by the reviewer", with the exact old text and the exact new text of the rule.
why: In version 1 the reviewer cannot check the owner's yes. The old and new text lets the owner decide quickly, and the line stops the packet from implying an approval.
applies-when: The PR changes a rule on the page together with its code.
not-when: none
example: In invented PR 101 for owner/shop, Not checked holds: approval: not checked by the reviewer, old: "R9. Orders can be cancelled within 24 hours.", new: "R9. Orders can be cancelled within 12 hours.".
mode: business, full
trigger: When writing the approval line for a changed rule
kind: user-policy
