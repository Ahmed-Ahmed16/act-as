id: REV-343
rule: Verify a callback's signature over the raw request body, before the framework parses it, and compare with a constant time function. Use no field until the signature is valid.
why: OWASP says the raw body must be given to the check before parsing, because changing the JSON in any way breaks the signature. A signature over the whole body also covers the fields that pick the order.
applies-when: A change adds or edits a webhook or payment callback route or handler.
not-when: The route is not a callback from another system.
example: In invented PR 101 for owner/shop, the callback reads $request->json('order_id') and marks the order paid, and the signature check runs on the re encoded JSON. The reviewer asks for the check on the raw body.
mode: security, full
trigger: When a changed route is a webhook or payment callback
kind: source-advice
source: OWASP Webhook Security Cheat Sheet, Signature Verification, https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Webhook_Security_Cheat_Sheet.html#signature-verification, read 2026-10-27
