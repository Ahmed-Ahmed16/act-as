id: REV-516
rule: When a few lines are added inside a long method, read the whole method and ask whether it now needs to be split because the change gave it a second job.
why: Google gives the case of 4 new lines that look fine in the diff but sit in a 50 line method that now needs to be broken up.
applies-when: The PR adds lines to a method that is already long, or the length pointer from health step 4 sends the reviewer here.
not-when: The added lines belong to the same job the method already does.
example: In invented PR 101 for owner/shop, 5 lines that send an email are added to a 90 line checkout method that already totals the cart. The reviewer writes that the method now has a second job and names both.
