id: REV-135
rule: Suggest a feature flag only when a part would show half a behaviour on its own. When you do, say to test the switch in the state it will have live.
why: A release toggle lets unfinished code ship switched off. The article (by Pete Hodgson, on Martin Fowler's site) says the setting you expect to go live in production is the one that matters most to test.
applies-when: A part, merged alone, would leave users with an unfinished screen or action, for example a loop of two parts broken by moving a piece to the later part.
not-when: The part is complete on its own, or the half state is invisible to users. Then suggest no flag.
example: In invented PR 101 for owner/shop, the reminder part sends mails with no continue link until the checkout part lands. The plan suggests a flag around the mail and says: test the switch in the state it will have live.
