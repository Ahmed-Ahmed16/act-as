id: REV-444
rule: When the PR changes a rule, search for a second copy of it and check that both copies still agree.
why: Kent Beck's design rules say every piece of knowledge should be said once. Hidden duplication is easy to miss, and two copies can drift apart when only one is edited.
applies-when: A changed hunk holds a rule (a limit, a status check, a price rule) that could live in more than one place.
not-when: none
example: In invented PR 101 for owner/shop, the PR changes the free shipping limit in the cart service from 100 to 150. The checkout view still shows a banner with 100. The reviewer reports the 2 copies disagree.
mode: health, full
trigger: When a rule in the PR may be copied in 2 places (L4)
