id: REV-229
rule: When a draft page is used, name it by its path and the SHA 256 of its text, keep a copy in the packet folder, and write "rules page: draft, not in the repo yet" in the business line.
why: The reader then knows which exact text was used, and the draft cannot change later without the hash showing it.
applies-when: A draft page is used for an area that has no page on the default branch.
not-when: none
example: In invented PR 101 for owner/shop, the reviewer used rules-drafts-owner-shop/gifts.md. The packet gives its path and SHA 256 and holds a copy in runs/.
mode: business, full
trigger: When a draft rules page is used
