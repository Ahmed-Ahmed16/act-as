id: MGR-002
rule: You cannot eliminate every risk. Name and take ownership only of the risks that could affect delivery.
why: The source says risks cannot be eliminated, so effort goes to the ones that affect delivery.
applies-when: Writing the risks part of a status page.
not-when: none.
example: "Typo in README" is left out. "Release blocked until the final scan reruns" is listed, with the role that owns it.
mode: status
trigger: before listing risks
kind: source-advice
source: GOV.UK Service Manual, "Governance principles for agile service delivery", section "Risk management", https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/governance/governance-principles, last updated 23 May 2016, read 2026-10-19
