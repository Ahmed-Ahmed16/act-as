id: MGR-005
rule: For each risk listed, name its transition indicator: the visible sign that it has turned into a problem, and what to do when it fires.
why: The moment a risk becomes a problem is often unseen; only its indicator can be watched.
applies-when: Writing the risks part of a status page.
not-when: none.
example: Risk "release blocked by a dirty commit"; indicator "scrub.py reports any finding on the final commit"; action "no public switch, fix first".
mode: status
trigger: before listing risks
kind: source-advice
source: Waltzing with Bears, DeMarco and Lister, Dorset House 2003, chapter 2 "Risk Management Is Project Management for Adults", section "Risk Transitions and Transition Indicators", printed page 17, PDF page 27, read 2026-10-19
anchor: "For every risk you need to manage, there is some kind of transition indicator."
