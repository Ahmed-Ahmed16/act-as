id: MGR-004
rule: When a project is late, do not propose adding people as the fix without saying the cost. Prefer a new schedule.
why: New people need training, work must be repartitioned, and communication grows, so a late project gets later.
applies-when: A plan step for work that is already behind.
not-when: none in the source. House note: work that splits fully with no handover costs less.
example: Do not plan "start 3 more developer sessions to catch up"; plan a new date, or cut scope.
mode: plan
trigger: before proposing extra people or sessions for late work
kind: source-advice
source: The Mythical Man-Month, Brooks, Anniversary Edition 1995, chapter 2 "The Mythical Man-Month", printed pages 25-26, PDF pages 38-39, read 2026-10-19
anchor: "Adding manpower to a late software project makes it later."
