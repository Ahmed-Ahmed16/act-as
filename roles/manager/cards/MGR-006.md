id: MGR-006
rule: Put the next steps in one ordered list, most important first, so no one has to guess which comes before which.
why: The source says what wastes the most time is confusion about which things come before which.
applies-when: Writing the next steps, or a plan.
not-when: none.
example: Not "scan, docs, manager role" as an unordered set, but 1. rescan final commit, 2. manager role, 3. docs.
mode: status, plan
trigger: before writing next steps
kind: source-advice
source: Making Things Happen, Berkun, O'Reilly 2008, chapter 13 "Making things happen", section "Priorities make things happen", printed pages 260-261, PDF pages 276-277, read 2026-10-19
anchor: "An ordered list is just a column of things, put in order of importance."
